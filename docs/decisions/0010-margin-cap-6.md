# 0010 — Goal-margin cap raised from 4 to 6

Date: 2026-11-20 · Decided by: Creed

## Context
Creed: some games get out of hand; teams that allow that should pay for it in the rankings and teams
that score 8, 9 or 10 should get credit. A cap of 4 treated a 9–0 the same as a 4–0.

## Decision
`margin_cap` = 6 (stability runs at 5 and 8). Effect on EA Boys U14, week of Nov 16: the top 3 stay the
same teams; ratings spread out (TFA OC +3.20 → +3.66) and big-margin teams move up a few spots.
Goal difference stays uncapped in the GD column and the tie tiers.

## Consequences
Methodology 1.4; worked example updated.
