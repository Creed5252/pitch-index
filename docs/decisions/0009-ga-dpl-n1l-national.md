# 0009 — GA, DPL and N1L national; N1L tiers kept apart

Date: 2026-11-20 · Decided by: Creed

## Context
GA and DPL were shown by conference, which repeats the league sites. N1L was shown by district.
N1L is very large and runs Premier 1 and Premier 2 tiers inside districts; mixing the tiers in one
ranking would be unfair and would draw complaints.

## Decision
- GA, DPL and N1L are always national (club-linked bridge where needed).
- GA and DPL get West / East region views. GA West = Frontier, Mountain West, Northwest,
  Pacific-Northwest, Southwest (GA's own West Regional list). DPL West = the conferences that played
  DPL's Western Regionals (Southwest, Southwest Open, Central Coast, Colorado, Mountain West, Mountain,
  Lonestar); the rest are East.
- N1L: Premier 1 and Premier 2 are separate national lists, never ranked together. Tier comes from the
  GotSport division name ("Premier 2", "Tier 2", "P2" = Premier 2; otherwise Premier 1, including
  districts that run one division or split only by geography). A conference filter (West, Northwest,
  South, Central, Midwest, Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, Northeast) narrows the list.
- Club links can now come from any table whose main group spans 2+ conferences with 20+ teams.

## Consequences
Methodology 1.3. N1L districts do not play each other in the regular season, so most N1L teams are
placed by estimate until the postseason; the page says so.
