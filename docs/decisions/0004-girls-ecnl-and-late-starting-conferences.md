# 0004 – Gap review before launch

Date: 2026-11-19

Creed asked for every data gap to be closed or explained before anything goes live. Findings:

- **ECNL Girls** runs 10 conferences in 2026-27 (Mid-Atlantic, Midwest, New England, North Atlantic, Northern Cal, Northwest, Ohio Valley, Southeast, Southwest, Texas). The boys-only conferences (Far West, Florida, Heartland, Mid-America, Mountain) do not exist for girls. Apparent "orphan" teams were last season's national playoff entries, which the season window already excludes. Name cleanup (spacing, " - " separators) added.
- **GA U15 and older**: Northeast and Pacific-Northwest schedules start in December and March; Mid-Atlantic North and Mid-America have played only a handful. Not a collection gap; those teams appear as their games are played.
- **N1L**: the first pass only reached groups linked from event home pages (262 of 765). Fetching every configured group directly raised scored games from 12,190 to 17,583. N1L is district-based, so it publishes by district until inter-district events connect it.
- **EA U15+**: Winter Qualifier not yet played; rankings publish now and will move after it.
